Wall of Text

A receipt printer on the wall.

Wall of Text is a small thermal printer on a home wall, run by a Raspberry Pi. People in the home use a web app on their phones to print notes, drawings and photos, and to schedule prints such as a morning briefing.

Google Calendar and Google Tasks

If you choose to connect a Google account, Wall of Text can print today's events from your Google Calendar and open tasks from your Google Tasks lists. It asks Google for read-only access and never creates, changes or deletes anything in your account.

Wall of Text is not a hosted service. Each printer runs on its owner's own Raspberry Pi at home, with a Google Cloud project that the owner creates for it. No company or server in between ever sees your calendar or tasks. See the privacy policy.
